# Birds Choice 4 Arm Complete Bird Feeder Pole Set with Squirrel Baffle

## What it is

A ground-mounted **pole + cylindrical baffle + 4-arm wire topper** kit sold on the Birds Choice store (handle includes “erva”; Shopify vendor **Erva**; details table brand **Birds Choice** — report both), not a seed hopper by itself. The squirrel defense on the manufacturer page is a black **14-1/2" cylindrical squirrel baffle** with a steel collar to hang it on the pole. The product page does not print baffle diameter, whether the baffle floats or rocks, or a per-arm weight limit. We did not measure it.

SKU **4 ARM POLE PKG**. UPC **789453700315**. Material steel. Mount type pole.

Distinct from the already-listed **Erva XL Cross-Arm** (**FPQSD**, **2"** shaft, **8** hang points on cross arms), from **Birds Choice Complete Pole Set with 1 Flange** (**POLE KIT 1 FL**, flange mounts not hanging arms), from **Birds Choice 2 Arm Complete** (**2 ARM POLE PKG**, two arms), from **Classic NP431** (feeder + pole + baffle bundle), and from **Squirrel Stopper Deluxe** (different brand station).

## Who it's for

People whose main problem is squirrels climbing a feeder pole, who want a Made-in-USA 1-inch steel pole with a printed cylindrical baffle and a four-arm topper for four hanging feeders, and who can plant a ground twister with a real jump-off buffer. Seed type and capacity are whatever you hang — this SKU is the pole, baffle, and arms. Cardinal perch is unknown; there is no feeder tray on the pole itself. Cornell FeederWatch still treats baffles plus about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual squirrel defense, and still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof gear.

## Who should skip

- You already have a working pole baffle and only need a feeder. Classic NP431 is the Birds Choice feeder-plus-pole-plus-baffle listing; Erva XL is the 2-inch multi-arm station; POLE KIT 1 FL is the single-flange kit; 2 ARM POLE PKG is the two-arm complete kit; Sky Cafe is the dome-style pole+baffle listing.
- You cannot keep about **10 feet** (Cornell) from a jump-off.
- You want a printed baffle diameter, a printed per-arm weight limit, or a multi-year warranty number before you buy. Those are unknown or only “satisfaction guaranteed” on the pages we fetched.
- You want a motorized spinner, a weight-activated tube, or a caged suet feeder as the main product. Those are different mechanisms. Earlier listings cover them. Motorized remains Flipper-only after tonight’s search.
- Your yard has bears. Cornell recommends against any feeding where bears are active except in hibernation season.
- You expected a seed hopper in the box. It is not included. **POLE KIT 2 FL** (two flanges) was not in the Birds Choice catalog we fetched tonight.
