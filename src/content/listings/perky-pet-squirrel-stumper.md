# Perky-Pet Squirrel Stumper Bird Feeder

## What it is

A hanging **caged seed** feeder from Perky-Pet, sold on the brand store as **Squirrel Stumper® Bird Feeder**, model **114G**. The mechanism on the manufacturer page is a **metal wire cage / barrier around the feeding ports** plus a **twist-lock lid** so squirrels are not supposed to open the top and empty the reservoir. The clear plastic reservoir holds **3 lb** of seed and has **eight** feeding ports spaced around the tube. Materials printed: **metal, plastic**. Powder-coated metal frame is claimed for rust and squirrel-chew resistance.

Printed specs on the Perky-Pet product page that day: Height **12.90 inches**; Diameter **6.2 inches**; Capacity **3 lb**; Food Type black-oil sunflower seed, mixed seed, safflower seed, and more. A second specs block on the same page prints Weight **1.3 lb** and Dimensions **12.9 in H × 5.75 in W × 6.2 in D**. Width **5.75** vs Diameter **6.2** on the same manufacturer page is left unresolved. UPC / GTIN unknown on that product page. Made-in country unknown on that page. Mount: hang.

This page is not a lab test. We did not hang one. Specs below are from the Perky-Pet product page (and its schema.org Product / Offer), one specialty-retailer page for price/stock/size cross-check only, and Cornell FeederWatch. We found no independent backyard test of model **114G** on Bob Vila, The Spruce, Birds & Blooms, Wirecutter, or Reviewed while researching tonight.

Distinct from already-listed caged seed pages: **Audubon NATUBE3** (plastic tube with ports inside a cage grid) and **Duncraft Squirrel Blocker** (mesh cylinder birds cling to). This one is a plastic hopper/tube with an outer wire barrier and eight ports. Distinct from the three caged **suet** listings.

## Who it's for

People who want a hang-mounted caged seed feeder for small songbirds, who like eight ports and a twist-lock lid, and who can keep a real jump-off buffer. Perky-Pet “Best Used For” list that day: chickadees, finches, nuthatches. That is a manufacturer attract list, not a census from our yard. Cardinal perch is **unknown** — cardinals are not on that Best Used For list. Cornell FeederWatch still uses about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual jump-off compromise, still notes wire-mesh cages sized for small birds as a real large-bird deterrent, and still treats baffles as usually the better squirrel defense than “squirrel-proof” marketing.

## Standouts

- Mechanism matches the type: a wire cage / barrier around the ports is the product’s squirrel claim, plus a twist-lock lid (manufacturer page).
- **Eight** feeding ports and **3 lb** capacity on the manufacturer table — useful if you want more ports than a four-port caged tube without jumping to a multi-station pole kit.
- Clear reservoir for seed-level checks (manufacturer copy).
- Perky-Pet store price **$25.49** and schema.org Offer `InStock` on **2026-09-18**. That is one store’s price and stock flag that day, not a survey.
- Powder-coated metal frame claim on the manufacturer page (durability claim, not a timed chew test we ran).

## Drawbacks

- Cage **opening size** is **unknown** on the Perky-Pet product page we fetched. Do not invent grid dimensions from photos. Without a printed opening size, you cannot verify whether a grey squirrel’s head or paw fits.
- Manufacturer page hosts mixed customer reviews (including reports that squirrels hang from the wire, reach lower ports, or defeat the lid). Those are store reviews, not an independent timed test. Schema.org `aggregateRating` on that page that day showed **ratingValue 2** with **reviewCount 22** — report the store schema figure; do not invent our own score.
- Dimension conflict left unresolved: Diameter **6.2 in** vs Width **5.75 in** on the same Perky-Pet page. Duncraft prints **6 × 13 inches tall** for the same model family — report both wordings; do not average.
- Price / stock conflict left unresolved: Perky-Pet **$25.49** / `InStock` vs Duncraft **$39.95** / backordered that day.
- Clearance wording conflict left unresolved: Perky-Pet admin replies on the product Q&A cite about **4 ft** up and **8 ft** (and in one older reply **12 ft**) from launching points; Cornell FeederWatch still uses about **10 feet**. Print both; do not invent a single official clearance for this SKU.
- Warranty years are thin: some Perky-Pet admin replies mention a **1-year replacement** / one-year replacement policy and a 30-day return. Not a multi-year warranty sheet on the product table. Contact printed in admin replies: **855-737-5973**.
- Made-in country unknown on the manufacturer product page we fetched.
- No independent timed test of **114G** turned up tonight.
- Manufacturer marketing still says “squirrel-resistant” / squirrel-proof framing. Cornell: squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders. Baffles plus jump distance still matter.

## Who should skip

- You need printed cage opening dimensions or a cardinal-friendly perch list before you buy. Opening size and cardinal perch are unknown / not on the Best Used For list.
- You cannot keep about **10 feet** (Cornell) from a jump-off — or you refuse to hang with the clearance Perky-Pet’s own admins describe.
- Your main problem is squirrels climbing a pole. That is a pole+baffle problem; earlier listings cover Classic NP431, Squirrel Stopper Deluxe, Erva XL, Birds Choice pole kits, and Sky Cafe.
- You want a motorized spinner or a weight-activated shroud/port closer as the main product. Those are different mechanisms (Yankee Flipper; Brome / Absolute / Roamwild family).
- You already have a working caged seed tube (NATUBE3) or mesh Blocker and only need suet — see the caged suet listings instead.
- Your yard has bears. Cornell recommends against any feeding where bears are active except in hibernation season.
- You expected a Made-in-USA claim or a multi-year warranty number on the product table. Those are unknown / thin on the pages we fetched.

## Verdict

If you want a sixth caged path and you can hang a Perky-Pet **114G** with a wire barrier, twist-lock lid, eight ports, and a real jump-off buffer, this is the Squirrel Stumper listing. No independent test of **114G** turned up tonight; manufacturer-hosted reviews are mixed. Cornell still wants distance (and often a baffle), and still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim. Buy the cage, the clearance, and the seed type together. Resistant, not magic.
