# Birds Choice Classic Squirrel Proof Bird Feeder with Pole and Baffle

## What it is

A pole-mounted tube feeder kit from Birds Choice, sold on the brand store as **Squirrel Proof Classic Bird Feeder with Pole and Squirrel Baffle**, SKU **NP431** (green) / **NP431RD** (red). UPCs printed on the store table: **789453300010** (green) and **789453004529** (red). The kit pairs a **6"** diameter × **12"** polycarbonate seed tube with a green baked-enamel coated steel baffle and top, four aluminum seed ports and perches, a metering ring, and **three 24"** steel pole sections. Store size line: **23"H w/baffle**; total assembled pole height **5 ft 8 in**. Store capacity: **approximately 5 lbs** of seed. Feed type on the store table: mixed seed, sunflower. Mount: pole. The store calls it the original “No-Squirrel Feeder!” and says Classic feeders have been tested in the field for over **30 years**. Brand About Us: headquartered in **Chilton, Wisconsin**; manufactures American-made bird feeders and houses; **100% satisfaction guaranteed**; since **2019** has kept millions of pounds of plastic out of landfills via recycled partners.

This page is not a lab test. We did not plant one. Specs below are from the Birds Choice product page (and its product JSON / schema.org offers), the manuals PDF linked under this product title, the Birds Choice About Us page, one specialty-retailer product page, and Cornell FeederWatch. We found no independent backyard test of SKU **NP431** on Bob Vila, The Spruce, Birds & Blooms, Wirecutter, or Reviewed while researching tonight.

## Who it's for

People whose main problem is squirrels climbing a feeder pole, who want a Classic tube, steel baffle/top, and pole in one box, and who can keep a real jump-off buffer. The store bird list is chickadees, cardinals, grosbeaks, titmice, jays, woodpeckers, doves, juncos, sparrows, and towhees. That is a manufacturer attract list, not a census from our yard. Cardinal perch is a real aluminum perch ring set (four ports), not a marketing adjective. Birds Choice did not print a jump-off distance on the NP431 page we fetched. Cornell FeederWatch still uses about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual jump-off compromise, still treats baffles under pole-mounted feeders as the usual squirrel defense, and still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders.

## Standouts

- Mechanism matches the type: the kit is a pole plus a steel baffle/top with the tube. Cornell’s usual advice is baffles beneath pole-mounted feeders; this SKU ships the pole and the steel baffle/top together (manufacturer claim).
- Printed kit geometry is concrete: **6" × 12"** tube, **23"H w/baffle**, three **24"** pole sections, total height **5' 8"**. Pound the narrowed end down with a rubber mallet or wood block + hammer (store).
- Four aluminum ports and perches. Metering ring with a printed best-practice seed level of **3–4 inches**.
- Made in the USA story is on the brand About Us page (Chilton, WI; American-made). Satisfaction guaranteed is brand-home language, not a numbered warranty PDF for this SKU.
- Fourth pole+baffle listing on this index after Arundale Sky Cafe (hang dome), Squirrel Stopper Deluxe (multi-arm station), and Erva XL Cross-Arm (multi-arm station). This one is a Classic single-tube kit with the pole in the box.

## Drawbacks

- Baffle diameter is **unknown** on the NP431 product page. Do not invent it from photos. Do not paste hanging-Classic polycarbonate baffle sizes onto this steel baffle/top.
- The manuals page links `BC_SquirrelProof_InstructionSheet_v3.pdf` under this product title. That PDF’s printed body describes a **hanging** 12" Classic with a **polycarbonate** weather baffle and a separate wrap-around “Classic Baffle” set **4–5 inches** above the perches, with hanging dims **12.5"H × 6"W** ( **17"H × 14.5"W** w/ hanger) and weight **2.7 lbs**, and “tested in the field for over **25 years**.” The NP431 product page itself prints steel baffle & top, pole sections, **23"H w/baffle**, table weight **4.5 lbs**, and “over **30 years**.” Leave the PDF/product mismatch unresolved. Do not treat the hanging sheet as NP431 steel-baffle diameter.
- Capacity wording conflict: Birds Choice store prints **approximately 5 lbs**. Wild Bird Store Online prints **1.2 gallons** in the prose and **5 lbs** in the specs on the same page. We are not converting gallons to pounds to “fix” that.
- Unit weight conflict: Birds Choice table **4.5 lbs**; Wild Bird Store Online **5 pounds**.
- Jump-off distance is not printed on the NP431 page. Cornell still wants about **10 feet** from trees or buildings, and a tilting baffle at least **18 inches** in diameter if squirrels jump from above. Print Cornell. Do not invent a manufacturer clearance.
- Manufacturer marketing still sells “squirrel proof” / “No-Squirrel.” Cornell: squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders. Baffles plus distance are usually the better defense.
- A numbered year warranty for NP431 is **unknown** on the product page and About Us page we fetched (About Us states **100% satisfaction guaranteed**; footer links a Warranty Intake page we did not treat as a printed term sheet for this SKU). Contact printed: birdlovers@birdschoice.com; 477 Vogt Lane, Chilton, WI 53014.
- Customer reviews on the manufacturer page are store reviews, not independent tests. Specialty-retailer comments are not a timed backyard trial. We are not scoring stars.

## Who should skip

- You only need a hanging dome over an existing feeder. Sky Cafe is the hang-dome pole+baffle listing.
- You want a multi-arm station for several hung feeders. Squirrel Stopper Deluxe and Erva XL Cross-Arm are the multi-arm listings.
- You cannot keep about **10 feet** (Cornell) from a jump-off. A pole baffle does not stop a leap onto the tube.
- You want printed baffle diameter or a printed jump-off distance before you buy. Those are unknown on the NP431 page we fetched.
- You want a motorized spinner, a weight-activated hang tube, or a caged suet feeder as the main product. Those are different mechanisms. Earlier listings cover them — including Birds Choice’s own WC13-SNDSF caged suet.
- Your yard has bears. Cornell recommends against any feeding where bears are active except in hibernation season.

## Verdict

If you want a fourth pole+baffle path and you can plant Birds Choice’s Classic tube-plus-steel-baffle on the included 5 ft 8 in pole with a real jump-off buffer, this is the Made-in-USA Classic kit listing. No independent test of NP431 turned up tonight. The manuals PDF linked for this title describes a hanging sibling; the product page is the pole kit. Cornell still wants baffles plus distance, and still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim. Buy the baffle, the clearance, and the refill habit together. Resistant, not magic.
