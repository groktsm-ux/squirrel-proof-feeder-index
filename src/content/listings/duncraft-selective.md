# Duncraft Squirrel Proof Selective Bird Feeder

## What it is

A hanging **caged seed tube** sold exclusively on the Duncraft store as the **Squirrel Proof Selective Bird Feeder**, SKU **171**, barcode / UPC **742933001715**. The manufacturer page describes a **seed tube surrounded by a durable wire mesh cage** that lets smaller songbirds feed inside a **fly-through cage** while limiting access by gray squirrels and many larger birds. Distinct design notes on that page: an **open-grid base** so loose seed and hulls fall through instead of collecting on a tray; **six** feeding ports; an **overhanging roof**; **squirrel-locking discs** on the roof; top-fill without disassembly; and a **5-1/2 inch** metal hanging cable.

## Who it's for

People who want a hang-mounted caged seed feeder for smaller songbirds, who prefer six ports inside a fly-through mesh cage, and who like an open-grid bottom so hulls do not pile on a tray attached to the feeder. Manufacturer bird-species table that day: chickadees, finches, wrens, titmice. That is a manufacturer list, not a census from our yard. Cardinal perch is **false** for this index — cardinals are not on that species table, and the cage is framed as limiting larger birds.
