# Erva XL Cross-Arm Pole System with Baffle

## What it is

A ground-mounted multi-station bird feeder **pole system** from Erva (Erva Tool & Manufacturing Co. Inc., Chicago), not a seed hopper by itself. The squirrel defense on the manufacturer page is an included **“Easy-install racoon-proof baffle”** (spelling as printed). The product page does not print baffle height, diameter, or whether the baffle floats, rocks, or is a fixed cylinder. We did not measure it. Do not borrow numbers from Erva’s separate Cylindrical Baffle (Round Pole) accessory — that accessory is listed for **1/2" to 1-3/4"** round poles, while this XL shaft is **2"**.

Printed specs on the Erva product page and Shopify product JSON that day: overall height **96"**; two cross arms at **34-1/2"** length combining for **8 hang points**; super-duty **2"** diameter galvanized steel center shaft; commercial-grade black powder-coat with matching black zinc-plated hardware; twist-in ground socket with installation lever; all hardware; detailed instruction sheet (PDF not linked on the page we fetched). Required tools: **2 crescent wrenches** or **2 wrenches** of **7/16"** and **1/2"**. Box size **60" × 10" × 10"**. SKU **FPQSD**. Barcode **0763945999920**. Vendor **Erva**. Made in USA. About Us: founded **1967**; manufactures in-house at **3100 W Grand Ave, Chicago IL 60622-4324**; sheet-metal rolling named for cylindrical and conical baffles.

This page is not a lab test. We did not install one. Specs below are from the Erva product page (and its product JSON), the Erva About Us page, one Erva cylindrical-baffle accessory tip for brand placement language only, and Cornell FeederWatch. We found no independent backyard test of SKU **FPQSD** on Bob Vila, The Spruce, Birds & Blooms, Wirecutter, or Reviewed while researching tonight.

## Who it's for

People whose main problem is squirrels climbing a feeder pole, who want a heavy USA-made multi-arm station with a baffle in the box, and who can plant a twist-in socket with a real jump-off buffer. Seed type and capacity are whatever you hang — this SKU is the pole and baffle. Cardinal perch is unknown; there is no feeder tray on the pole itself. Cornell FeederWatch still treats baffles plus about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual squirrel defense, and still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof gear.

## Standouts

- Mechanism matches the type: a pole baffle is the product. Cornell’s usual advice is baffles under pole-mounted feeders; this kit ships a raccoon-proof baffle with the pole (manufacturer claim).
- **8 hang points** on two cross arms (**34-1/2"** arm length printed). Same hang-point count as the earlier Squirrel Stopper Deluxe listing, different brand and shaft story.
- **2"** galvanized center shaft and “heaviest” / “super-duty” framing for wind chimes, flower baskets, and heavy feeders (product blurb). Per-hook weight limit is **unknown** on the page we fetched.
- Made in USA in Chicago. About Us names in-house stamping, welding, and sheet-metal rolling for baffles. That is company history, not a lab proof of this SKU.
- Tools and hardware are named up front (wrenches required). Useful if you want a bolted build rather than a twist-lock tube story.
- Erva store price **$249.99** and Shopify `available: true` on **2026-09-09**. Free shipping called out on the product blurb. That is one store’s price and stock flag that day, not a survey.

## Drawbacks

- This is not a feeder. You buy seed capacity with whatever you hang. Empty `seedTypes` on purpose.
- Baffle size unknown on the XL product page. Height, diameter, and motion style are not printed there. Do not invent them from photos. Do not paste the separate cylindrical-baffle accessory’s **14-1/2"–28"** height / **6-1/4"–7-1/2"** diameter / **1/2"–1-3/4"** fit range onto a **2"** XL shaft.
- Per-hook and total weight limits unknown on the product page we fetched.
- Warranty for this SKU is **unknown** on the product page and About Us page we fetched. Contact printed: support@erva.com; factory address above.
- Placement is not printed on the XL product page. Erva’s separate cylindrical-baffle accessory tip says place feeders **8–10'** from railings, roofs, or trees, and keep baffles at least **4'** off the ground. Cornell FeederWatch still uses about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual jump-off compromise, and wants a tilting baffle at least **18 inches** in diameter if squirrels jump from above. Print both. Do not merge them.
- Manufacturer marketing still sells a “racoon-proof” / squirrel-deterrent station. Cornell: squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders. Baffles are usually the better defense, and jump distance still matters.
- Instruction sheet is mentioned but not linked as a downloadable PDF on the product page we fetched. We did not reverse-engineer the sheet from photos.
- Customer reviews on the XL product page: **none** that day (“Be the first to write a review”). Do not treat Multi-Arm Pole System reviews as reviews of FPQSD.
- Product page HTML also showed a “Sale Sold out” badge while Shopify JSON said `available: true` and Add to cart was present. Leave that UI conflict unresolved; report the JSON availability flag and the price we saw.

## Who should skip

- You only need one hanging tube and already have a working pole baffle. Sky Cafe is the dome-style pole+baffle listing; Squirrel Stopper Deluxe is the other multi-arm station already listed.
- You cannot keep about **8–10 feet** (Erva tip) / about **10 feet** (Cornell) from a jump-off.
- You want printed baffle dimensions, a printed per-hook weight limit, or a printed warranty before you buy. Those are unknown on the pages we fetched.
- You want a motorized spinner, a weight-activated tube, or a caged suet feeder as the main product. Those are different mechanisms. Earlier listings cover them.
- Your yard has bears. Cornell recommends against any feeding where bears are active except in hibernation season.
- You expected a seed hopper in the box. It is not included.

## Verdict

If you want a third pole+baffle path and you can plant a 96-inch Made-in-USA 2-inch multi-arm pole with Erva’s included raccoon-proof baffle and a real jump-off buffer, this is the USA-factory multi-arm listing. No independent test of FPQSD turned up tonight. Cornell still wants baffles plus distance, and still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim. Buy the baffle, the clearance, and the hung feeders together. Resistant, not magic.
