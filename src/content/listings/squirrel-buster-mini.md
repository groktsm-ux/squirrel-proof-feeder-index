# Brome Squirrel Buster Mini

## What it is

A compact hanging tube feeder from Brome. The US store calls it Mini but mighty, with a **1 lb** seed capacity and **4 seed ports** for clinging and perching birds. The mechanism matches the larger Squirrel Buster line: openings in the shroud line up with the ports for birds; extra weight on the feeder forces the shroud down and closes access. Brome labels this model **NON-ADJUSTABLE** — unlike the Plus, there is no spring-weight dial on the product page.

This page is not a lab test. We did not hang one. Specs below are from Brome’s product page and one independent 1-week test of eight feeders.

## Who it's for

People who want a smaller hang tube for songbirds in limited space, and who do not need the Plus’s larger capacity or adjustable spring. Bob Vila’s tester used it for songbirds including cardinals and titmice. Brome’s placement copy is the same family as Plus: window, tree, deck, fence, garden, house, rain trough, or sliding doors, with **18 inches** of clearance around it.

## Standouts

- Same named mechanism as Plus: squirrel weight closes the ports. Bob Vila found the tension spring worked reliably when hung with clearance.
- Compact. Brome says **1 lb**. Bob Vila’s comparison table lists **0.6 quarts** for the same model and the Mini review also says “about a pound.” We are not converting those units. Both numbers are on the linked pages.
- Four metal perches. Bob Vila says testing attracted all songbirds, including cardinals and titmice. Brome’s bird list on the Mini page includes Northern Cardinal.
- Chew-proof claim on parts squirrels can reach. Bob Vila also notes RoxResin components like the full-size model.
- Seed Tube Ventilation at the top, same family claim as Plus.
- Detachable by hand, no tools, for cleaning. Cornell FeederWatch wants seed feeders cleaned about every week or two.
- Lifetime Care is Brome’s support line, Monday–Friday 9 a.m.–5 p.m. EST, except holidays. That is a phone promise, not a measured lifespan.
- Bob Vila named it Best Bang for the Buck after an eight-feeder, one-week test. That is a price-and-performance pick in a short test, not a durability study.
- Bob Vila also noted the smaller body leaves less area for a squirrel to grab onto.

## Drawbacks

- Brome’s own disclaimer: this feeder will not deter grackles, starlings, juvenile red/Douglas/flying squirrels, chipmunks, possums, bears, deer, or raccoons. They tell you to bring it in at night.
- **NON-ADJUSTABLE.** If you need selective weight settings for larger songbirds vs squirrels, Brome points you elsewhere in the line (Plus is the adjustable sibling already listed).
- Small capacity. Bob Vila refilled it a couple of times per week in their test.
- If a squirrel can reach the ports without putting its weight on the feeder, the shroud does not close. Brome wants at least 18 inches of clearance. Cornell FeederWatch still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders and that baffles plus about 10 feet from a jump-off are usually the better defense.
- Bob Vila tested eight feeders for one week. That is short. “Best bang” is a useful data point, not a multi-season durability study.
- Hang only as the primary mount on these sources. Do not treat it as a pole system.
- Brome’s US store listed **$36.95** on 2026-09-04. That is one store’s price that day, not a survey. We are not ranking value.
- Height and exact dimensions are unknown on the Brome Mini product page we fetched. Retailer pages that invent inches are not used here.

## Who should skip

- You need adjustable spring tension or Plus-scale capacity. Get the Plus listing, not this one.
- You cannot hang it with roughly 18 inches of clear air around it.
- Your problem is grackles or starlings. Brome says this model does not stop them.
- You need a caged suet feeder, a motorized spinner, or a pole-and-baffle dome. Those are different mechanisms (already listed elsewhere on this site).
- You live with bears. Cornell FeederWatch recommends against feeding except when bears hibernate.
- You hate frequent refills. Mini is the small end of the Brome tube line.

## Verdict

If you want Plus-style weight-activated hang hardware in a smaller, cheaper, non-adjustable package, this is Bob Vila’s Best Bang pick from a one-week, eight-feeder test. The mechanism is real. Brome publishes the animals it will not stop and marks the spring non-adjustable. Cornell still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim squirrels often beat. Buy it as resistant hardware plus placement, not as a spell.
