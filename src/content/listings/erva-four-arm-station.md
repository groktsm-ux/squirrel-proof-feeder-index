# Erva Squirrel-Proof Four Arm Bird Feeding Station

## What it is

A ground-mounted four-arm bird feeder **pole station with a pole baffle**, not a seed hopper by itself. Sold on the Duncraft store as **Squirrel-Proof Four Arm Bird Feeding Station**, SKU **TREE3**; the Shopify vendor and schema.org Brand on that page are both **Erva Tool & Die Company**, and the variant barcode / schema GTIN is **763945321820**. The squirrel defense is a **16 inch long** squirrel baffle on the pole. Duncraft prints four arms with **22 inch** outreach ("the widest arm outreach of any station we carry"), up to **20 lbs. per arm**, a sectional pole with a **21 inch** twister ground socket, all-steel with black powder coat, **90 inches** tall above the ground with a **10 inch** finial topper, no-tool assembly, and **Made in the USA**. Feeders are sold separately.

Erva's own store does not list a SKU called TREE3, and barcode **763945321820** did not appear in Erva's product feed tonight. The closest Erva catalog page is **Multi-Arm Bird Feeder Pole Systems**, whose **Extended Reach / 4** variant (**FP5TERN4**, **$109.99**) prints **90"** ground to finial, **~22"** extended reach, a **20"** twist-in ground socket, **16 gauge** steel, and Made in USA — but Erva sells baffles there as separate accessories. Whether TREE3 is that pole plus a baffle is **unknown**. The socket length differs between the two pages (Duncraft **21 inch**, Erva **20"**); we leave both.

Distinct from already-listed pole+baffle gear: **Erva XL Cross-Arm** (eight hooks, heavier pole), **Squirrel Stopper Deluxe** and **Sequoia**, Birds Choice **POLE KIT 1 FL** / 2-arm / 4-arm / Classic NP431, **Duncraft Double Delight**, and **Sky Cafe**. Not Classic, not a Brome shroud, not Absolute.

This page is not a lab test. We did not install one. Specs below are from the Duncraft product page (and its schema.org / Shopify data), Erva's Multi-Arm pole page, and Cornell FeederWatch. We found no independent timed backyard test of TREE3 on Bob Vila or The Spruce while researching tonight. Store star ratings are not independent tests — we do not score them.

## Who it's for

People whose main problem is squirrels climbing a feeder pole, who want four wide-spaced arms for wide feeders (Duncraft: "We love this feeding station for hanging your widest feeders!"), and who can plant the socket with a real jump-off buffer. Seed type and capacity are whatever you hang — this SKU is the pole, arms, and baffle. Cardinal perch is **unknown**; there is no feeder tray on the station. Cornell FeederWatch treats baffles under pole-mounted feeders plus about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual squirrel defense, and says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders.

## Standouts

- Mechanism matches the type: a pole baffle is included in the Duncraft copy ("Squirrels can't climb past the 16 inch long squirrel baffle").
- Four arms with **22 inch** printed outreach — useful if you hang wide hoppers that would bump a narrow cross-arm.
- **20 lbs. per arm** printed load. Erva's XL Cross-Arm prints more per arm, but this is the lighter four-arm option.
- Made in the USA on both the Duncraft page and Erva's Multi-Arm page.
- No-tool assembly and twist-in ground socket printed.
- **$179.95** on the Duncraft store on **2026-10-05** (one store, one fetch day; not a price survey).

## Who should skip

- You want eight hanging points or a heavier pole — look at **Erva XL Cross-Arm** or **Squirrel Stopper Deluxe**, already listed.
- You need a seed hopper with a weight shroud, cage, or motorized spinning perch. Different buckets (Yankee Flipper remains the only honest motorized listing).
- You need it delivered now — Duncraft showed it backordered tonight.
- You want Birds Choice **POLE KIT 2 FL** — still no live manufacturer page tonight (404).
- You cannot keep about **10 feet** of jump-off clearance (Cornell).
- You live with bears. Cornell FeederWatch recommends against feeding except when bears hibernate.

## Verdict

Tenth pole+baffle listing if you want a four-arm, wide-reach Erva station with a 16-inch baffle — lighter and cheaper than the eight-hook Erva XL already listed, not a Squirrel Stopper, and not Classic. Resistant, not magic; backordered on the one store that sells it tonight, and no independent test found.
