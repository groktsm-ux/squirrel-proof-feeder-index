# Droll Yankees Yankee Flipper

## What it is

A hanging tube feeder from Droll Yankees, sold on the Perky-Pet store as model YF-M. The store lists a 5 lb capacity for black-oil sunflower or mixed seed, four ports, and a circular perch. The mechanism is a weight-activated motorized perch ring: extra weight on the ring starts a motor, the ring spins, and the squirrel is tossed off. The store says the ring is calibrated for large birds such as cardinals and woodpeckers. The tube is UV-stabilized polycarbonate with powder-coated metal parts. Power is a NiCad battery inside a Power Stick. The box includes an AC/DC charger. Bob Vila measured the feeder at 21 inches. The manufacturer spec table on Perky-Pet does not list height.

This page is not a lab test. We did not hang one. Specs below are from the Perky-Pet product page, the Yankee Flipper instruction PDF, and one independent 1-week test of eight feeders.

## Who it's for

People who want a motorized hang feeder and will take the base indoors to charge it. The store's bird list is cardinals, chickadees, finches, grosbeaks, jays, nuthatches, titmice, and woodpeckers. That is a manufacturer attract list, not a census from our yard. It is a hang feeder. Perky-Pet's placement copy is at least 4 feet off the ground and 8 feet from anything a squirrel can jump from. The instruction PDF wants a minimum of 14 inches from a deck rail, pole, or tree so the squirrel has to put its full body weight on the ring, and says a greater distance may be better. Cornell FeederWatch still uses about 10 feet from trees or buildings as the usual jump-off buffer.

## Standouts

- Mechanism is named and visible: squirrel weight spins the perch. The instruction PDF is explicit that a squirrel leaning from a nearby rail, with only part of its weight on the ring, may not trigger it.
- Bob Vila tested eight feeders for one week. They named Yankee Flipper "best large," called it the most robust model in that lineup, and said it worked extremely well to keep squirrels off. That is one week, not a durability study.
- Capacity is 5 lb on both the Perky-Pet spec table and Bob Vila's comparison table. No unit conflict on this model.
- Four ports and a circular perch. The store says even large birds can feed. Cardinal perch is a real ring, not a marketing adjective.
- Internal baffles (store) / internal baffle (Bob Vila) to keep seed moving to the ports.
- Lift-off lid and wide mouth. The store recommends cleaning at least every two weeks. Cornell FeederWatch wants seed feeders cleaned about every week or two.
- The instruction PDF documents disassembly, Power Stick charging (minimum 14 hours, not more than 24), and a Lifetime Warranty against squirrel damage, with written exclusions.

## Drawbacks

- The product is the motor. If the Power Stick is dead, the ring does not spin, and it is an ordinary tube. Bob Vila noted the motor must be charged regularly, depending on squirrel activity, with the included AC adapter. They also found the filled feeder heavy enough to droop a shepherd's hook. The PDF wants a rigid hook; falls are not covered under the squirrel-damage warranty.
- Perky-Pet's own asterisk: a "100% squirrel-proof" feeder starts with hanging it correctly. The PDF adds that red squirrels and chipmunks may not be heavy enough to activate the ring. Cornell still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders, and that baffles plus about 10 feet from a jump-off are usually the better defense.
- Power Stick uses NiCad batteries. Charge indoors, at room temperature, only when the ring no longer throws squirrels. Overcharging and repeated undercharging are called out in the PDF. Do not immerse the base, Power Stick, or charger. Do not open the Power Stick; opening voids warranties; parts inside are not replaceable.
- Lifetime Warranty against squirrel damage is not a blank check. The PDF excludes tube cracks from overtightened screws (heat or ice expansion) and excludes falls. Perky-Pet store admins, answering public questions in 2024–2026, said they do not sell replacement parts, including Power Sticks for older YF-M units.
- Raccoons and other large animals can drain the batteries. The PDF tells you to bring the feeder in at night until they go elsewhere. Cornell FeederWatch recommends against feeding at all where bears live, except when bears hibernate.
- Turn the black knob OFF before charging, filling, cleaning, or if freezing rain is in the forecast.
- Perky-Pet listed **$99.99** on 2026-08-30. That is one store's price that day, not a survey. We are not ranking value.
- The current Droll Yankees domain (drollyankees.com) is a More Birds storefront. The working product page we fetched is on Perky-Pet. We did not invent a founding year.

## Who should skip

- You will not take the base inside and charge a NiCad Power Stick on a 14–24 hour cycle when the spin fades.
- You cannot hang it so a squirrel must put full weight on the ring (nearby roof, railing, or branch).
- Your problem is red squirrels or chipmunks that may be too light for the ring, or raccoons that will sit on it all night.
- You need a caged suet feeder or a pole-and-baffle system as the main product. Those are different mechanisms. Later listings will cover them.
- You live with bears. Cornell FeederWatch recommends against feeding except when bears hibernate.
- You want a feeder whose wearing parts the current store will sell you. Perky-Pet's own Q&A says they do not sell replacement parts.

## Verdict

If you want a motorized hang feeder, you will charge it, and you will hang it so a squirrel cannot lean in, this is the default motorized listing. Bob Vila's one-week test found it kept squirrels off. The instruction PDF is honest about light squirrels, lean-in hangs, raccoons, and battery care. Cornell still treats "squirrel-proof" as a claim squirrels often beat. Buy it as a motor plus placement, not as a spell.
