# Roamwild PestOff Squirrel Proof Bird Feeder 2.0

## What it is

A hanging tube feeder from Roamwild, sold on the US brand store as **PestOff Squirrel Proof Bird Feeder 2.0** (sunflower / mixed-seed model — not the Nyjer/thistle SKU, not the peanut/mealworm SKU, not Compact, not Mega). Mechanism: **individually spring-loaded, weight-activated ports**. The store says when a heavier animal lands on one perch, **only that port closes**; birds on the other port keep feeding; the port reopens when the weight leaves. The page also names **dual squirrel-proof baffles**, an **anti-squirrel ring**, factory pre-set springs (no calibration), integrated rain guards, a **riot shield grade** tube, and **stainless steel** baffles. Spec table on the product page: **Height 26"**, **Width 7"**, empty **Weight 1.5 lbs**, **Seed capacity 4 lbs**, **2 feeding ports**, UV resistant, steel hanging mount included. Compatible seed list on the page: black oil sunflower, sunflower hearts, safflower, sunflower chips, striped sunflower, mixed seed. Seed is not included. Price on the US page **$49.99** on **2026-09-14**, with free standard US shipping from a Columbus, Ohio warehouse. Finishes named: Heritage Green, Antique Platinum, Antique Gold, Antique Copper, USA Flag.

This page is not a lab test. We did not hang one. Specs below are from the Roamwild US PestOff 2.0 product page, the Bird Feeders collection page, the Our Story page, Cornell FeederWatch, and a check of Bob Vila’s eight-feeder squirrel-proof test (PestOff is not on that list). We found no independent backyard test of PestOff 2.0 on Bob Vila, The Spruce, Birds & Blooms, Wirecutter, Reviewed, or Consumer Reports while researching tonight. Store star ratings and customer comments are manufacturer-store reviews, not independent timed tests — we do not score them.

## Who it's for

People who want a weight-activated hang tube that closes **per port** instead of dropping a single shroud over every port (the Brome Squirrel Buster / Solution style already listed elsewhere), and who will hang it with real clearance. The store bird attract copy names cardinals, chickadees, and finches (sunflower hearts recommended). That is a manufacturer attract list, not a census from our yard. Mount: hang on a pole, branch, or shepherd’s hook (store). Clearance printed on the product page: **at least 18 inches** from any vertical surface (wall, fence, post, trunk) and **60 inches from the ground**. Cornell FeederWatch still uses about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual jump-off compromise, still treats baffles plus distance as the usual squirrel defense, and still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders.

## Standouts

- Mechanism is named and distinct from the five weight-activated listings already on this index (Brome Plus, Mini, Solution 200; WoodLink Absolute / Absolute II): individually sprung ports, not one shared shroud or perch-ring calibration story. Manufacturer claim only — we did not verify the patent claim in a patent database tonight.
- Printed capacity is concrete: **4 lbs** on the product page and on the collection comparison table.
- Dual stainless-steel baffles + anti-squirrel ring + spring ports are listed together as the squirrel story on the FAQ (“three defenses”).
- Factory pre-set / no calibration is repeated across product FAQ and comparison copy — useful if you do not want a spring dial.
- Cleaning: store says the feeder separates by hand, no tools, rinse with warm water, under 60 seconds. Cornell FeederWatch wants seed feeders cleaned about every week or two.
- Brand home: Our Story — founded in the UK in **1990** by Lloyd Freedman; US home **Columbus, Ohio**; family business. Contact printed: info@roam-wild.com.
- Warranty language on the product page (fetched tonight): **5 year** warranty and **60 day** money-back / PestOff Promise if instructions were followed and squirrels still win. That is brand copy, not a PDF terms sheet we archived.

## Drawbacks

- Height conflict on the same product page: the specifications table prints **Height 26"** (and FAQ “Hung on its hanger it measures about 26 inches tall”), while the “How is the PestOff 2.0 different…” FAQ says it **stands 21 inches tall**. We are not picking a winner. Leave both.
- Only **two** ports. The store’s own comparison pitch tells you to hang it near a tree/bush and watch birds take turns. If you need six ports, this is the wrong SKU family.
- Cardinal perch is **unknown**. Manufacturer attract copy includes cardinals. At least one store customer review on the same product page says the lever closes when cardinals land and asks about weight adjustment (the page also says no calibration). We leave that conflict unresolved. Do not treat this as a proven large-perch cardinal feeder.
- Manufacturer marketing still sells **“squirrel proof” / 100% squirrel-proof**. Cornell: squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders. Roamwild’s own 18-inch / 60-inch clearance notes matter; Cornell’s ~10-foot jump-off buffer is stricter than 18 inches from a trunk.
- No independent timed backyard test of this SKU turned up tonight. Roamwild homepage “As Featured In” logos are marketing placements, not a Bob Vila / Spruce / Wirecutter protocol we can cite for pass/fail.
- Customer reviews on the manufacturer page are store reviews. Mixed comments on the page we fetched include spills, fill difficulty, raccoons breaking a perch, and squirrels still winning for some buyers. Those are anecdotes, not a scored trial.
- Hang-primary. Do not treat this listing as a pole-and-dome baffle system (Sky Cafe / Stopper / Erva / NP431 cover that type).
- Price **$49.99** on **2026-09-14** is one store’s price that day, not a survey. We are not ranking value.
- Numbered SKU / UPC for the generic 2.0 seed-tube page is **unknown** on the page we fetched (color variants have separate URLs; we did not invent a master SKU).

## Who should skip

- You want a Brome-style adjustable shroud or the Absolute perch-weight story already listed — different mechanism details, different brands.
- You need more than two ports, or a nyjer / peanut / Mega capacity SKU. Those are separate Roamwild pages, not this listing.
- You cannot hang it with about **18 inches** clear of vertical surfaces (Roamwild) and you ignore Cornell’s ~**10 feet** jump-off advice.
- You need a proven independent backyard test before you buy. None found for PestOff tonight.
- You mainly want guaranteed cardinal feeding without port closure. Treat cardinal use as unproven here.
- You want a motorized spinner, a caged suet feeder, or a pole+baffle kit as the main product. Those are different mechanisms (already listed elsewhere — Flipper is still the only motorized spinning-perch listing).
- Your yard has bears. Cornell recommends against any feeding where bears are active except in hibernation season.

## Verdict

If you want a sixth weight-activated hang path and you specifically want **individually spring-loaded ports** with dual steel baffles — not another Brome Classic/Plus/Mini/Solution near-dupe and not Absolute — this is the Roamwild PestOff 2.0 seed-tube listing. No independent test turned up tonight. Height 26" vs 21" is unresolved on the manufacturer page. Cornell still wants distance plus baffles, and still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim. Buy the springs, the clearance, and the refill habit together. Resistant, not magic.
