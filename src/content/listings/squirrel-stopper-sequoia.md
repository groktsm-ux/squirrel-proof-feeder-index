# Squirrel Stopper Sequoia

## What it is

A ground-mounted multi-station bird feeder **pole system** from **Squirrel Stopper LLC**, not a seed hopper by itself. Sold on the Duncraft store as **Squirrel Stopper Sequoia**, SKU **SG20** (schema.org Brand and Shopify vendor on that page: **Squirrel Stopper LLC**). Also sold on JCS Wildlife as **Squirrel Stopper Sequoia Squirrel Proof Bird Feeder Pole System with 4 Hanging Stations**; JCS Shopify SKU / barcode field that day was **082045034530** (treat as UPC-style barcode; Duncraft has no barcode on the variant). The squirrel defense is a pre-attached **floating** / **Universal** baffle on the middle pole section: Duncraft calls it a patented **27-inch** all-metal “floating” Squirrel Stopper baffle with black powder-coated finish; JCS cites patented baffle technology **US007574976B2** that moves up and down and side to side. Same company as the already-listed **Squirrel Stopper Deluxe** — JCS says so on the Sequoia page. We did not measure the baffle.

Printed / schema specs that day (leave height and hang-height conflicts unresolved):

- Duncraft: stands **90 inches** tall above the ground; feeders hang between **five and six feet**; **4** hangers hold up to **15 lb** each; pole **1-1/4 inch** diameter **16 gauge** tubular steel; screw-in ground auger; easy assemble without tools; price **$199.95**; schema Offer **InStock**; visible page also said **backordered**; Shopify variant weight **0.0 lb**.
- JCS: height above ground **8 ft 2 in**; hangers / plants “hanging between **7-6** feet off of the ground” (page phrasing); **4** hooks; **19"** space between hangers and pole; each arm up to **15 lb**; pole **1-1/4 in** diameter **16 gauge** tubular steel; install **10 feet** from poles or walls; **manufactured in China**; price **$168.88**; shipping weight **16.0 lb** on the Shopify variant.

Distinct from already-listed pole+baffle gear: **Squirrel Stopper Deluxe** (eight hanging points / cross tubes), Birds Choice **POLE KIT 1 FL** / 2-arm / 4-arm / Classic NP431, **Erva XL Cross-Arm**, and **Sky Cafe**. Sequoia is the **four-hanger** Squirrel Stopper station with the floating baffle — not Classic, not a Brome shroud, not Absolute.

This page is not a lab test. We did not install one. Store star ratings and customer comments are not independent timed tests — we do not score them.

## Who it's for

People whose main problem is squirrels climbing a feeder pole, who want one four-station ground pole with a floating cylinder baffle (not the eight-point Deluxe), and who can plant the auger with a real jump-off buffer. Seed type and capacity are whatever you hang — this SKU is the pole and baffle. Cardinal perch is **unknown**; there is no feeder tray on the pole itself. Cornell FeederWatch still treats baffles plus about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual squirrel defense, and still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders. JCS also prints **10 feet** from poles or walls on the Sequoia page — print both Cornell and JCS; do not invent a third clearance.

## Standouts

- Mechanism matches the type: a pole baffle is the product. Duncraft / JCS both sell a pre-attached floating / Universal baffle as the squirrel defense.
- Four hanging stations with printed **15 lb** per hanger (Duncraft bullets; JCS “each arm will hold up to 15lbs”). Simpler station than Deluxe’s eight points if you only need four hooks.
- Pole construction printed on both stores: **1-1/4"** diameter **16 gauge** tubular steel; Duncraft “much stronger than wrought iron” framing is manufacturer marketing.
- JCS prints patent **US007574976B2** and Made in China; Duncraft prints the **27-inch** baffle length. Useful numbers in writing before you buy.
- JCS shipping weight **16.0 lb** that day (Duncraft Shopify weight was **0.0 lb** — leave both).

- Height conflict left unresolved: Duncraft **90 inches** vs JCS **8 ft 2 in**. Hang-height conflict left unresolved: Duncraft **5–6 feet** vs JCS “**7-6** feet” phrasing. Report both pages; do not average.
- Price conflict left unresolved: Duncraft **$199.95** vs JCS **$168.88** on **2026-09-30**. One store each, not a survey.
- Stock conflict left unresolved on Duncraft: visible **backordered** vs schema.org Offer **InStock** / Shopify `available: true`.
- Product weight conflict left unresolved: Duncraft Shopify **0.0 lb** vs JCS Shopify **16.0 lb**.
- Seed capacity is N/A — empty `seedTypes` on purpose. You buy capacity with the feeders you hang.
- Cardinal perch unknown; no perch geometry on the pole SKU.
- Manufacturer “squirrel-proof” / “with no exceptions” framing is a claim. Cornell: squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders; jump distance still matters.
- No independent timed test of Sequoia / SG20 tonight. The Spruce’s tested list picks **Squirrel Stopper Deluxe** as the pole system, not Sequoia.
- Bob Vila’s eight-feeder squirrel-proof roundup did not surface Sequoia in tonight’s check.

## Who should skip

- You want the eight-point **Squirrel Stopper Deluxe** already listed — different SKU, more hangers.
- You need a seed hopper with a weight shroud, cage, or motorized spinning perch. Different buckets (Flipper remains the only honest motorized spinning-perch listing).
- You want Birds Choice **POLE KIT 2 FL** — still no live manufacturer product page tonight (404 / absent from catalog).
- You need a proven independent backyard test of Sequoia before you buy. None found tonight.
- You cannot keep ~**10 feet** of jump-off clearance (Cornell and JCS).
- You live with bears. Cornell FeederWatch recommends against feeding except when bears hibernate.

## Verdict

Eighth pole+baffle listing if you want Squirrel Stopper’s four-hanger Sequoia with the floating cylinder baffle — not the eight-point Deluxe already listed, and not Classic. Resistant, not magic; no independent timed test of SG20 found tonight.
