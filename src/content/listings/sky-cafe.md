# Arundale Sky Cafe

## What it is

A platform / hopper feeder from Arundale Products (Sky Cafe) whose main squirrel defense is a steep 17-inch dome baffle over the seed tray. Squirrels that land from above are supposed to slide off. The brand store and Bluebird Landing both describe a 1-1/2 gallon seed bucket under a clear-view hopper that dispenses onto a circular tray. Duncraft, selling the same feeder, says the hopper holds up to 10 lb of seed and measures 17 inches diameter by 17 inches tall. We are not converting gallons to pounds. Both numbers stay.

Mounting is either hang or pole. Duncraft’s clear model includes a 24-inch hanging chain. ThinkOutsideNatureCo sells a separate cord-and-lock pole mount kit for a 1-inch OD pole and does not include the metal pole. Bluebird Landing and ThinkOutside also sell a feeder-plus-pole-baffle kit that adds a Squirrel-Away pole baffle for climbs from below. Made in the USA. Arundale advertises a lifetime warranty against squirrel damage.

This page is not a lab test. We did not hang one. Specs below are from the Arundale brand store pages, one specialty retailer product page, one specialty catalog page, one independent ~1-month backyard test of eight feeders, and Cornell FeederWatch.

## Who it's for

People whose main problem is squirrels climbing or dropping onto a feeder, and who want a dome (and optionally a pole baffle) instead of a spring, motor, or cage. Arundale and Bluebird Landing both name cardinals as a fit for the wide circular tray; Duncraft’s body copy also names grosbeaks and cardinals on the 2-inch tray, while its species table lists chickadees, finches, and nuthatches. Print all of that. Birds & Blooms’ Katie Bandurski, testing eight feeders for about a month outside Milwaukee, called this her most successful squirrel option once she found a mount with enough clearance for the wide baffle.

## Standouts

- Mechanism matches the type: a dome baffle is the product. Cornell FeederWatch still treats baffles plus distance from a jump-off as the usual defense, and this feeder is built around that idea.
- Birds & Blooms’ tester wrote that squirrels and chipmunks tried hard with little success, that the top baffle blocks access from above, and that there was no useful climb from the sides or below on her hang setup. That is one yard, about one month.
- Wide tray aimed at shy large songbirds. Arundale and Bluebird Landing both say cardinals that hesitate on narrow perches will use this platform.
- Capacity is large on every page we fetched. Arundale / Bluebird Landing / ThinkOutside: 1-1/2 gallon bucket. Duncraft: up to 10 lb. Infrequent fill is the shared claim.
- Hang or 1-inch pole. Separate Squirrel-Away pole baffle exists for below-climb protection when you pole-mount.
- Polycarbonate (Arundale / ThinkOutside, material update dated 2015) or 1/8-inch recycled plexiglass (Duncraft). Both stores say USA-made and high impact. We are not picking a winner on the plastic name.
- Lifetime warranty against squirrel damage is Arundale’s claim on the brand pages. Duncraft’s own store guarantee is one year from purchase. Different sellers, different warranties.

## Drawbacks

- Clearance is non-negotiable. Birds & Blooms’ tester said the baffle was so wide it tilted her usual shepherd’s hook; she needed a longer arm or an arch / branch with space on all sides.
- Bigger mammals beat the dome from below if they can reach the tray. The same Birds & Blooms tester discontinued use after deer or raccoons cleaned it out in minutes and said she needed a spot at least eight feet off the ground. That is not a squirrel failure. It is a height and species limit.
- Cornell’s hanging / above-baffle note asks for a tilting baffle at least 18 inches in diameter. This dome is listed at 17 inches. We are not rounding up. Print both.
- Cornell still wants about 10 feet from trees or buildings as the usual jump-off buffer. A dome does not cancel placement.
- Capacity numbers conflict across stores (1-1/2 gallon vs up to 10 lb). Material wording conflicts (polycarbonate vs recycled plexiglass). Leave both.
- Pole is not in the box on the pole-mount kit pages we fetched. You buy a 1-inch OD pole separately.
- Manufacturer marketing still says “squirrel proof.” Cornell FeederWatch: squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders. Birds & Blooms opens the same way: there is no such thing as completely squirrel-proof.
- Duncraft listed **$99.95** on 2026-09-01 and showed the clear feeder backordered. Bluebird Landing listed the feeder at **$129.99** and feeder-plus-pole-baffle kits at **$179.99** that day. ThinkOutside’s feeder-plus-pole-baffle page showed sold out with a messy strike-through price block ($134.99 / $164.99). Those are one-store prices that day, not a survey.

## Who should skip

- You cannot give the 17-inch dome room to hang level, or you only have a short-arm shepherd’s hook. Birds & Blooms hit that exact failure.
- Your yard has deer, raccoons, or bears that can reach an elevated tray. Birds & Blooms lost the feeder to larger mammals; Cornell recommends against any feeding where bears are active except in hibernation season.
- You want a weight-activated tube, a motorized spinner, or a caged suet feeder as the main product. Those are different mechanisms. Earlier listings cover them.
- You need a printed single capacity number before you buy. Stores disagree on gallons vs pounds.
- You expect the pole and the below-climb baffle in every SKU. Hang-only Duncraft pages include a chain; the below baffle and the metal pole are separate purchases on the pages we fetched.

## Verdict

If you want a pole-and-dome style resistant feeder and you can mount the wide baffle with real clearance, this is the default pole + baffle listing. Birds & Blooms’ one-month backyard test called it the most successful of eight against squirrels and chipmunks. The same test shows the failure modes: bad mount geometry, and mammals tall enough to ignore the dome. Cornell still wants baffles plus about 10 feet from a jump-off, and still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim. Buy the dome and the placement together.
