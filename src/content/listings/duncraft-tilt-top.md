# Duncraft Squirrel-Proof Tilt-Top Bird Feeder

## What it is

A hanging **weight-tilt dome / platform** feeder sold on the Duncraft store as the **Duncraft Squirrel-Proof Tilt-Top Bird Feeder**, SKU **790**, GTIN / barcode **742933007908**. Schema.org Brand and Shopify vendor that day are both **Duncraft**. The manufacturer page describes a **Tilt-Top squirrel baffle**: the roof **tilts steeply when a squirrel steps on**, sending them slip-sliding toward the ground, while birds fly up under the dome to perch and feed. ## Who it's for

People who want a hang-mounted seed bowl under a clear tilting dome, who like watching birds fly up under the baffle, and who accept a weight-triggered tilt as the squirrel defense rather than a closed port shroud or wire cage. Manufacturer bird-species table that day: cardinals, bluebirds, grosbeaks. That is a manufacturer list, not a census from our yard. Cardinal perch is **unknown** for this index — cardinals appear on the species table and the page describes a perching area under the dome, but we did not verify perch geometry or whether a cardinal feeds comfortably. Cornell FeederWatch still uses about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual jump-off compromise, still treats baffles plus distance as the usual squirrel defense, and still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders.

- Mechanism is named: weight on the dome **tilts** the Tilt-Top baffle steeply so squirrels slip off (Duncraft product page). Distinct from Brome shroud, Absolute perch dial, PestOff springs, and Nature's Way collapsing sleeve already listed.
- Printed **2-inch** gap under the dome and **16-inch** clearview baffle give a see-through watch angle the manufacturer emphasizes.
- Capacity **2 to 3 lb** of any seed; top-fill; easy-clean framing on the product page. Cornell FeederWatch wants seed feeders cleaned about every week or two.
- Made in the USA / Duncraft-exclusive claim on the product page; introduced-**1979** history claim is on that page (manufacturer marketing, not our verification).

- You want another Brome shroud, Absolute, PestOff, or Nature's Way collapsing-shield story — already listed; this is a tilting-dome bowl story.
- You need a wire **caged** exclusion feeder (Selective, Blocker, Heavy-Duty Caged, Stumper, etc.). Different bucket.
- You need a motorized spinning perch. Flipper is still the only honest motorized spinning-perch listing on this index.
