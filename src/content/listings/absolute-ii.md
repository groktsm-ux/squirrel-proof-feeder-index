# WoodLink Absolute II

## What it is

A double-sided metal hopper from WoodLink (Heritage Farms Absolute II, item 23802/7536). The manufacturer page calls it a squirrel-resistant feeder with pole and hanger. The defense is weight-sensitive perches on the front and back: enough weight on a perch closes access to the seed openings. WoodLink says the perches can be adjusted to exclude larger birds as well as squirrels. The reservoir holds 8 quarts of safflower or mixed blends. Clear plastic side windows show the seed level. The roof swings up for one-handed fill or clean and latches on one side. Made in the USA. Manufacturer dimensions: 12.000 in L × 14.250 in W × 9.250 in H. Listed shipping weight on that page: 7.500 (units not named). UPC on that page: 047977003979.

Mounting is hang or pole. BirdHousesAndBaths, selling the same Absolute II name with matching 12 × 14.25 × 9.25 in body, includes a black metal pole (58 in), plastic ground socket, plastic adapter, and a hanger for a hook. That store also prints capacity as **8 qts., 12 lbs** on one line. We are not converting quarts to pounds. Both numbers stay.

This is not the same SKU as WoodLink Absolute item **24640**. That sibling page (Cloudflare challenged one fetch; prior-night browser plus search snippets still usable) describes a double-sided feeder with spring-activated perches, a no-waste seed-saver baffle, up to **12 lb** capacity, and **no pole in the box**. Different item number, different capacity wording, baffle wording Absolute II’s manufacturer page does not use. Many 7536 retailer pages paste Absolute-style copy (12 lb, seed-saver baffle, 16 × 13.5 × 11.25 in) onto Absolute II titles. Print the collisions. Do not merge the SKUs.

This page is not a lab test. We did not hang one. Specs below are from the WoodLink Absolute II product page, one specialty Absolute II retail page that matches those dimensions, one specialty Absolute II price line, the sibling Absolute 24640 page for the SKU split, Bob Vila’s 1-week Absolute (not Absolute II by name) test of eight feeders, and Cornell FeederWatch.

## Who it's for

People who want a weight-activated metal hopper they can pole-mount or hang, with birds feeding from both sides. BirdHousesAndBaths names cardinals among the species list for Absolute II, along with chickadees, doves, finches, grosbeaks, jays, nuthatches, sparrows, titmice, woodpeckers, and others. That is retailer copy, not a census. Seed types on that page: cracked corn, mixed seed, peanut chunks, safflower, sunflower kernels, sunflower seed.

## Standouts

- Mechanism is named: weight on the perch closes the seed openings. Adjustable to cut larger birds as well as squirrels (WoodLink Absolute II page).
- Double-sided. Two fronts for birds instead of a single tube shroud.
- Pole and hanger are part of the Absolute II kit story on WoodLink and on BirdHousesAndBaths. Hang or stand.
- Capacity is large on every Absolute II page we trust for the model: WoodLink **8 quarts**; BirdHousesAndBaths **8 qts., 12 lbs**. Infrequent fill is the shared claim.
- All-metal body (WoodLink). BirdHousesAndBaths adds speckled green powder-coated finish.
- Made in the USA on WoodLink and BirdHousesAndBaths.
- Clear windows for seed level (WoodLink).

## Drawbacks

- Capacity wording conflicts. Manufacturer Absolute II: **8 quarts**. BirdHousesAndBaths Absolute II: **8 qts., 12 lbs**. Bird Watchers Supply and Gifts Absolute II line: **12 pounds** at **$139.00**. Many 7536 store pages use Absolute dimensions and Absolute baffle copy. Leave all of that unresolved.
- SKU mix-ups are common. Absolute **24640** is a different product (12 lb, seed-saver baffle, no pole on that manufacturer page). Buying “Absolute” on a best-of list is not automatic proof you got Absolute II.
- Bob Vila’s Best Post pick is titled **WoodLink Absolute Squirrel-Resistant Bird Feeder**, not Absolute II. That 1-week, 8-feeder test used a 12 lb double-sided metal feeder with a 5-foot pole and “weighted perch with baffles” in the comparison table. Squirrels stayed out most of the time; a few still swiped handfuls. Heavy enough that the tester preferred pole-mount over hang. That is evidence about Absolute (named Absolute), not a named Absolute II trial. We did not invent an Absolute II lab result.
- Cornell FeederWatch: squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders. Baffles plus about 10 feet from a jump-off remain the usual defense. A weight perch does not cancel placement.
- Manufacturer marketing still says squirrel-resistant / squirrel-proof language across the line. We say resistant.
- WoodLink Absolute II page showed no price on fetch day. BirdHousesAndBaths Absolute II: **$101.88** (struck **$107.24**). Bird Watchers Supply Absolute II: **$139.00**. One-store prices that day, not a survey.
- JCS Wildlife’s Absolute II 7536 page was backordered at **$102.96** and used Absolute-sized dimensions (16 × 13.5 × 11.25 in) plus seed-saver baffle copy. Treat that page as a collision example, not a clean Absolute II spec sheet.

## Who should skip

- You need a single clean capacity number before you buy. Stores and the factory disagree on quarts vs pounds, and Absolute vs Absolute II pages collide.
- You want Bob Vila’s Exact Best Post SKU by name. That write-up says Absolute, 12 lb, baffles. This listing is Absolute II.
- You only hang on a light shepherd’s hook. Even related Absolute testing called the filled metal hopper heavy and better on a pole.
- You want a motorized spinner, a caged suet cage, or a dome baffle as the main product. Those are different mechanisms. Earlier listings cover them.
- Your yard has bears. Cornell: do not feed except when bears hibernate.
- You expect a seed-saver baffle because a retailer pasted Absolute copy onto an Absolute II title. WoodLink’s Absolute II page does not use that baffle wording.

## Verdict

If you want a USA-made double-sided weight-activated metal hopper with a pole kit, Absolute II is the honest next weight-activated listing after Plus. Buy by item **23802/7536** and read the capacity line twice. Treat Absolute **24640** and Absolute II as separate products. Bob Vila’s Absolute week is useful color on the family, not a substitute Absolute II test. Cornell still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim. Resistant, with placement still required.
