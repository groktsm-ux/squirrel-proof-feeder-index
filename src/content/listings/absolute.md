# WoodLink Absolute

## What it is

A double-sided all-metal hopper from WoodLink sold as **Absolute Squirrel-Resistant Feeder (No Box, No Pole)**, item **24640**, UPC **093432246405**. Manufacturer dimensions on that page: **13.500 in L × 16.000 in W × 11.300 in H**. Shipping weight on that page prints **0.000** (units not named). Treat weight as unknown from the factory sheet.

The defense is metal spring-activated / counter-balance perches on both sides, adjustable to three positions (light, medium, heavy). WoodLink: the wrong bird or squirrel closes access to seed; when a squirrel steps onto the perch, a metal shield covers the seed tray and shuts off the supply. The top locks down. A **no-waste seed-saver baffle** is part of this Absolute page’s copy (Absolute II’s manufacturer page does not use that baffle wording). Capacity on WoodLink: **up to 12 lbs** of mixed seed, sunflower seeds, peanut halves, or safflower. Seed-level indicator window. Unlatch the roof and lift to fill or clean. Overhanging roof and hopper keep seed dry. All-steel body on that page.

Mount story for **this** SKU: the WoodLink title is **No Box, No Pole**. Toledo Indoor Garden (MPN **24640**, same UPC) lists a metal hanger included and does not claim a pole in the box. Farm and Home Supply sells manufacturer number **24640** under the title **Absolute III**, with hanger included and a mounting plate **not included** for pole mount. Bob Vila’s Best Post pick is titled **WoodLink Absolute Squirrel-Resistant Bird Feeder** and describes a kit with a **5-foot mounting pole** and hanging hardware. Pole-in-box vs no-pole is unresolved across those pages. Mount on this listing is hang or pole (either), with the factory 24640 sheet saying no pole in that carton.

This is not Absolute II (**23802/7536**). Absolute II’s WoodLink page is a different item number, different dimensions (**12.000 × 14.250 × 9.250 in**), and capacity **8 quarts**, with pole and hanger in that Absolute II kit story. Do not merge Absolute, Absolute II, and Absolute III titles.

This page is not a lab test. We did not hang one. Specs below are from the WoodLink Absolute 24640 product page, two specialty Absolute/Absolute III retail pages that cite 24640, Bob Vila’s 1-week Absolute (by name) test of eight feeders, Cornell FeederWatch, and the Absolute II sibling page only for the SKU split.

## Who it's for

People who want a large double-sided weight-activated metal hopper and will buy by item **24640**, not by the Absolute / Absolute II / Absolute III name alone. WoodLink seed list on Absolute: mixed seed, sunflower, peanut halves, safflower. Farm and Home Absolute III (24640) adds cracked corn, peanut chunks, sunflower kernels, and sunflower seed to its seed list. Cardinal perch is unknown on the manufacturer Absolute page; we did not invent one.

## Standouts

- Mechanism is named: weight on the perch closes the seed openings / metal shield covers the tray (WoodLink Absolute 24640). Adjustable light / medium / heavy.
- Double-sided. Two fronts.
- Seed-saver baffle is on the Absolute 24640 manufacturer page.
- Capacity is large on WoodLink Absolute: **up to 12 lbs**. Infrequent fill is the shared claim with Bob Vila’s Absolute write-up.
- Locking top plus all-steel body on WoodLink.
- Bob Vila Best Post (Absolute by name): 1-week, 8-feeder test. Squirrels stayed out most of the time; a few still swiped handfuls. Large capacity, infrequent filling. Ratings on that page for Absolute: Ease of Use 4.7/5, Effectiveness 4.5/5, Durability 5/5, Value 4.5/5. That is a named Absolute trial, not Absolute II.

## Drawbacks

- Capacity and naming collide. WoodLink Absolute 24640: **up to 12 lbs**. Farm and Home Absolute III (manufacturer number 24640): center chamber **8 quarts**, overview line **8 qts., 12 lbs**, and title Absolute **III**. Leave quarts vs pounds and Absolute vs Absolute III unresolved.
- Dimensions collide. WoodLink Absolute: **13.5 × 16 × 11.3 in**. Farm and Home Absolute III 24640: **14 × 11.25 × 9.5 in**. Print both.
- Pole kit collides. WoodLink Absolute title: **No Box, No Pole**. Toledo: hanger included. Farm and Home: hanger included; mounting plate not included. Bob Vila Absolute Best Post: comes with a **5-foot** pole and hanging hardware. We did not invent which carton Bob Vila opened.
- Material wording collides. WoodLink Absolute: all-metal / all-steel. Bob Vila Absolute table: alloy steel and polycarbonate. Leave both.
- UPC on WoodLink Absolute: **093432246405**. Farm and Home Absolute III row: UPC **400204908230** with manufacturer number still **24640**. Same MPN, different store UPC line — leave it.
- Factory shipping weight prints **0.000**. Unknown.
- Made in the USA appears on Farm and Home Absolute III copy. WoodLink Absolute 24640 description we fetched does not print that phrase. Leave USA origin as retailer claim for that store page, not as a factory Absolute line we can quote.
- Cornell FeederWatch: squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders. Baffles plus about 10 feet from a jump-off remain the usual defense. A weight perch does not cancel placement. Clean seed feeders every week or two. Bears: do not feed except when they hibernate.
- Manufacturer and retail pages still say squirrel-proof / squirrel-resistant. We say resistant.
- Prices that day (one store each, not a survey): Toledo Indoor Garden Absolute 24640 **$132.99** (in stock). Farm and Home Absolute III 24640 **$59.99**. WoodLink Absolute page showed no price on fetch.

## Who should skip

- You need one clean name, one capacity number, and one dimension set before you buy. Absolute / Absolute II / Absolute III pages disagree.
- You assumed item **24640** includes the 5-foot pole Bob Vila described. The WoodLink Absolute 24640 title says no pole.
- You only hang on a light shepherd’s hook. Bob Vila preferred pole-mount for Absolute because the filled metal hopper is heavy.
- You want Absolute II’s pole-and-hanger kit by item **23802/7536**. That is a different listing.
- You want a motorized spinner, a caged suet cage, or a dome baffle as the main product. Those are different mechanisms. Earlier listings cover them.
- Your yard has bears. Cornell: do not feed except when bears hibernate.

## Verdict

If you want Bob Vila’s Best Post Absolute by name, this is the matching weight-activated SKU to buy carefully: item **24640**, UPC **093432246405** on WoodLink, knowing that carton is labeled **No Pole** and that Absolute III store pages reuse the same manufacturer number with different quarts, inches, and UPCs. Treat Absolute II as a sibling, not a synonym. Cornell still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim. Resistant, with placement still required.
