# Nature's Way Squirrel Shield Advantage Cage Feeder

## What it is

A hanging tube feeder sold on the Nature's Way Bird Products store as the **Squirrel Shield Advantage Cage Feeder**, model **SPCAGE-1**, UPC **850028089354**. Schema.org brand string that day is **Squirrel Shield™**; the store and Our Story page are **Nature's Way Bird Products** — report both. The squirrel defense on the manufacturer page is a **weight-sensitive seed shield that collapses under a squirrel's weight** to block seed-port access, plus a **quick-locking / quick-lock roof**. Duncraft's reseller page for the same model names a **sliding metal sleeve that shuts all eight seed ports simultaneously** and **collapsing perches**. We did not hang one, so we do not pick which wording is "truer."

Printed specs on the Nature's Way product page (and its schema.org Product / Shopify JSON) that day: **8 feeding ports**; capacity **3.2 quarts**; dimensions **19.25"H × 7"W × 7"D**; page weight **2.4 lbs**; powder-coated metal roof and cage with UV-stabilized plastic feeder; seed list black oil sunflower, striped sunflower, hulled sunflower, safflower, mixed seed blends. Mount type hang. Price **$70.99**. Schema Offer **InStock**.

Distinct from already-listed weight-activated hang feeders (Brome Plus / Mini / Solution 200; WoodLink Absolute / Absolute II; Roamwild PestOff) and from Perky-Pet Squirrel Stumper (caged hang). The metal "cage" / lantern frame is real on the page, but the manufacturer attract list includes **cardinals**, so this is not a classic small-gap cage that excludes squirrels by mesh size alone. Mechanism bucket for this index: **weight-activated**.

This page is not a lab test. Specs below are from the Nature's Way product page, Nature's Way Our Story, one Duncraft reseller page for capacity/mechanism cross-check, Cornell FeederWatch, and a check of Bob Vila's eight-feeder squirrel-proof roundup (SPCAGE-1 is not on that list). Store star ratings and customer comments are manufacturer-store reviews, not independent timed tests — we do not score them.

## Who it's for

People who want a weight-activated hang feeder with a collapsing seed shield / sleeve and eight ports, from a brand not already on this index, and who will hang it so a squirrel must put weight on the perches rather than lean in from a rail. Manufacturer bird attract list: cardinals, grosbeaks, titmice, nuthatches, chickadees, juncos, finches. That is a manufacturer list, not a census from our yard. Cardinal perch is **unknown** — attract copy includes cardinals; we did not verify perch geometry. Cornell FeederWatch still uses about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual jump-off compromise, still treats baffles plus distance as the usual squirrel defense, and still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders.

- Eight ports and a printed **3.2 qt** capacity on the manufacturer page — useful if you want more stations than a two-port PestOff.
- Quick-lock / push-toggle roof and tool-free easy-clean base are printed on Nature's Way (and echoed on Duncraft). Cornell FeederWatch wants seed feeders cleaned about every week or two.
- Powder-coated metal roof and cage + UV-stabilized plastic tube are named on the manufacturer page.

## Who should skip

- You want another Brome shroud or Absolute perch-weight story — already listed; this is a different brand and a collapsing shield/sleeve story.
- You need a classic small-mesh **caged** exclusion feeder (Audubon NATUBE3, Duncraft Blocker, Stumper, etc.). Cardinals on the attract list mean this is not that bucket.
- You need a motorized spinning perch. Flipper is still the only honest motorized spinning-perch listing on this index.
- You cannot hang it so a squirrel must load the perches (nearby roof, railing, or branch).
- You need a proven independent backyard test before you buy. None found for SPCAGE-1 tonight.
- You live with bears. Cornell FeederWatch recommends against feeding except when bears hibernate.
- You want a pole-and-baffle kit as the main product. Those are different listings.

## Verdict

If you want a seventh weight-activated hang path and a **Nature's Way / Squirrel Shield** collapsing seed-shield tube with eight ports — not another Brome or Absolute near-dupe — this is the SPCAGE-1 listing. Capacity 3.2 qt vs Duncraft 3 lb is unresolved. Stock UI on the manufacturer page disagrees with itself; schema said InStock that day. No independent timed test turned up tonight. Cornell still wants distance plus baffles, and still treats "squirrel-proof" as a claim. Buy the shield, the hang, and the refill habit together. Resistant, not magic.
