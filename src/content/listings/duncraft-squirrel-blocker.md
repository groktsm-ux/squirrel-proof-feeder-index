# Duncraft Squirrel Blocker Mesh Feeder

## What it is

A hanging **caged seed** feeder sold exclusively by Duncraft as the **Squirrel Blocker™ Bird Feeder**, SKU **1462**, GTIN / barcode **742933014623**. The mechanism is a stainless-steel mesh cylinder with an **outer mesh guard**: songbirds cling and feed through the wire; the outer grid is supposed to keep a squirrel’s nose and paws off the seed. Duncraft copy: fill with black-oil sunflower, shelled peanuts, whole sunflower hearts, suet nuggets, or dried mealworms; holds **1-1/2 lbs** of sunflower seed; **5-1/4 inch** diameter; **16-3/8 inches** tall; steel hanger; top fill with squirrel lock; green metal.

This page is the **base** Blocker, not the “with Guard” or “with Slot Top Baffle” kits (those are separate Duncraft URLs). This page is not a lab test. We did not hang one. Specs below are from the manufacturer product page, one editorial brand roundup that names Blocker, Bob Vila’s eight-feeder / one-week test (which did **not** include this SKU), and Cornell FeederWatch.

## Who it's for

People who want a **caged seed** feeder after NATUBE3’s tube-in-cage design — this one is mesh birds cling to, not a plastic tube with ports inside a grid. Duncraft’s spec table bird list for the base SKU: **Finches, Chickadees**. Seed list on the body copy is broader than the spec table’s “Black Oil Sunflower Seed” feed-type field. It hangs. Cardinals are not on the base product’s bird list; the related with-Guard and Slot-Top kits list Cardinals on *their* pages. Cornell FeederWatch still uses about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual jump-off buffer, and treats baffles as usually the better squirrel defense than “squirrel-proof” marketing. Manufacturer clearance distance for this SKU is **unknown** on the product page we fetched.

## Standouts

- Second **caged seed** listing on this index (Audubon NATUBE3 was the first seed-tube cage; prior other caged pages are suet). Mechanism class matches site type **caged**; design is mesh cylinder + outer guard, not NATUBE3’s ABS tube inside a powder-coated grid.
- Named manufacturer facts: SKU **1462**, capacity **1-1/2 lbs** sunflower, dimensions **5-1/4" × 16-3/8"**, stainless mesh that “helps aerate” seed, outer guard for squirrel reach, top fill with squirrel lock, exclusive at Duncraft, one-year product guarantee / 365-day satisfaction language on the same store.
- Bob Vila’s eight-feeder / one-week test did not include Blocker — so we are not borrowing Flipper / Brome / Absolute scores for this SKU.
- Avian Enthusiasts’ 2026 Duncraft roundup describes Blocker as double-layer mesh aimed at clinging birds (chickadees, nuthatches, woodpeckers in that editorial framing) and notes perching cardinals may struggle. That is one editorial summary, not a timed backyard lab we re-ran. Their affiliate widget text “18834 … Blocker Selective” conflicts with manufacturer SKU **1462** — leave that naming mess; we keep **1462**.

## Drawbacks

- Availability copy conflicts on the same fetch day. Visible product page: “backordered and will ship as soon as it is back in stock.” Structured data Offer: **InStock**; Shopify variant `available: true`. Do not pick a winner.
- Price is one store one day: Duncraft sale **$59.95** (compare-at **$69.95**). No second reliable retailer listing for SKU 1462 found the same day; manufacturer says exclusive.
- Spec bird list is narrow (Finches, Chickadees) while body seed list includes peanuts, hearts, suet nuggets, mealworms. Leave both.
- Related kits change dimensions / bird lists / price — do not merge them into this SKU. With Guard: **$79.95**, 12" weather guard, table dims **5-1/4" × 12" × 16"**, bird list Cardinals / Finches / Nuthatches. Slot Top Baffle kit: sale **$99.95** / **$114.90**, 16" slot-top guard, similar table dims, height also printed “16 inches tall.”
- No Bob Vila / Birds & Blooms / Spruce timed test of SKU 1462 in sources we opened. Store star ratings ignored as evidence.
- Cardinal perch is not a ring on this product. `cardinalPerch: false`.
- Cornell FeederWatch: squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof feeders (photo caption shows a squirrel inside a “squirrel-proof” cage); clean seed feeders about every week or two; if you live with bears, do not feed except when bears hibernate. Wire-mesh cages that admit only small birds also keep desirable larger birds out.

## Who should skip

- You want cardinals as primary guests on a perch ring. Base Blocker is cling mesh; cardinals are not on Duncraft’s base bird list.
- You need a motorized spinner. Flipper is still the only true battery-motor spinning-perch listing on this index; no second motorized SKU with primary sources tonight.
- You want a plastic seed **tube** inside a cage (NATUBE3 class) or a weight-activated hang tube or a pole+baffle kit as the main product. Those are different designs / mechanisms.
- You need same-day ship and will not accept a backorder. Visible Duncraft copy said backordered on fetch day even while schema said InStock.
- You live with bears. Cornell FeederWatch recommends against feeding except when bears hibernate.

## Verdict

If you want a second **caged seed** option after Audubon’s NATUBE3 tube-in-cage, Duncraft’s Squirrel Blocker (SKU 1462) is the mesh-cling + outer-guard answer on the manufacturer’s own store. Capacity and size are printed cleanly on that page. Stock status and related-kit bird lists disagree across pages. Cardinals are not the target on the base SKU. Cornell still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim squirrels often beat. Buy it as mesh plus placement, not as a spell.
