# Birds Choice Complete Pole Set with 1 Flange and Squirrel Baffle

## What it is

A ground-mounted **pole + cylindrical baffle + one mounting flange** kit from Birds Choice (Chilton, Wisconsin), not a seed hopper by itself. The squirrel defense on the manufacturer page is a black **14-1/2" cylindrical squirrel baffle** with a steel collar to hang it on the pole. The product page does not print baffle diameter, whether the baffle floats or rocks, or a per-flange weight limit. We did not measure it.

Printed specs on the Birds Choice product page (and its Shopify product JSON) that day: **3-section** heavy-duty **16 gauge** wall, **1" diameter** black steel tubing; ground **twister** that twists into the ground **20"** with a set screw and **1/2"** turning holes; black **14-1/2"** cylindrical squirrel baffle; **1** flat topped mounting flange; **Pole is 60"**; **Holds 1 bird feeder** on the pole. Package list: twister, squirrel baffle, steel collar, **28"** base pole section with no swedges, **14"** pole section with one swedge (**actual extension is 12"**), **22"** pole section with one swedge (**actual extension is 20"**), and 1 flat topped mounting flange. SKU **POLE KIT 1 FL**. UPC **789453700261**. Made in the USA. Vendor string on Shopify: **BirdsChoice**. Store handle includes the word “erva” and tags include **Erva**; this page still ships as a Birds Choice store SKU — do not merge it with the separate Erva XL Cross-Arm listing (**2"** multi-arm FPQSD).

This page is not a lab test. We did not install one. Specs below are from the Birds Choice product page, Birds Choice About Us, one reseller page for price/height/weight cross-check only, and Cornell FeederWatch. We found no independent backyard test of SKU **POLE KIT 1 FL** on Bob Vila, The Spruce, Birds & Blooms, Wirecutter, or Reviewed while researching tonight.

Distinct from the already-listed **Birds Choice Classic NP431**, which is a Classic tube feeder bundled with pole and baffle. This SKU is the pole kit alone.

## Who it's for

People whose main problem is squirrels climbing a feeder pole, who want a Made-in-USA 1-inch steel pole with a printed cylindrical baffle and a single flange for one medium or large feeder / platform, and who can plant a ground twister with a real jump-off buffer. Seed type and capacity are whatever you mount — this SKU is the pole, baffle, and flange. Cardinal perch is unknown; there is no feeder tray on the pole itself. Cornell FeederWatch still treats baffles plus about **10 feet** from trees or buildings as the usual squirrel defense, and still says squirrels often find a way into so-called squirrel-proof gear.

## Standouts

- Mechanism matches the type: a pole baffle is the product. Cornell’s usual advice is baffles under pole-mounted feeders; this kit ships a cylindrical squirrel baffle with the pole (manufacturer claim).
- Printed baffle height **14-1/2"** on the manufacturer page (diameter unknown on that page). Useful if you want a number in writing before you buy.
- Complete kit: twister, three pole sections with named extensions, baffle collar, and **1** flat flange for one feeder. No scavenger hunt for a matching flange.
- **1"** 16-gauge steel, Made in the USA (product page). About Us: headquartered in **Chilton, Wisconsin**; American-made feeders and houses; **100% satisfaction guaranteed** (company claim, not a timed lab of this SKU).
- Birds Choice store price **$158.99** and Shopify variant `available: true` on **2026-09-17**. That is one store’s price and stock flag that day, not a survey.

## Drawbacks

- Baffle **diameter** and motion style (fixed vs floating) are **unknown** on the Birds Choice product page we fetched. Do not invent them from photos. Cornell wants a tilting baffle at least **18 inches** in diameter if squirrels jump from above — we cannot confirm this baffle’s diameter from the page.
- Per-flange and total weight limits unknown on the product page we fetched.

## Who should skip

- You already have a working pole baffle and only need a feeder. Classic NP431 is the Birds Choice feeder-plus-pole-plus-baffle listing; Erva XL and Squirrel Stopper Deluxe are multi-arm stations; Sky Cafe is the dome-style pole+baffle listing.
- You cannot keep about **10 feet** (Cornell) from a jump-off.
- You want a printed baffle diameter, a printed per-flange weight limit, or a multi-year warranty number before you buy. Those are unknown or only “satisfaction guaranteed” on the pages we fetched.
- You want a motorized spinner, a weight-activated tube, or a caged suet feeder as the main product. Those are different mechanisms. Earlier listings cover them.
- Your yard has bears. Cornell recommends against any feeding where bears are active except in hibernation season.
- You expected a seed hopper in the box. It is not included. Buy the two-flange kit if you need a seed-catcher platform plus a hopper on the same pole — that is a different SKU (**POLE KIT 2 FL**) and is not this page.

## Verdict

If you want a fifth pole+baffle path and you can plant a Made-in-USA 1-inch Birds Choice pole kit with a printed **14-1/2"** cylindrical baffle, one flange, and a real jump-off buffer, this is the single-flange complete pole listing. No independent test of **POLE KIT 1 FL** turned up tonight. Cornell still wants baffles plus distance, and still treats “squirrel-proof” as a claim. Buy the baffle, the clearance, and the mounted feeder together. Resistant, not magic.
